SENTRA · FINAL-YEAR PROJECT

Privacy notice

Version: 18 November 2026

Purpose and participation

Sentra uses supported consumer wearable data to develop and evaluate wellness and fitness-for-shift views. Its indicators are provisional, not diagnoses or medical-grade measurements. Participation is voluntary. You choose your sharing level and may withdraw without giving a reason. Existing participants keep their current agreement until they confirm a new version.

Information used

With your authorization, Sentra receives supported activity, heart and sleep readings from Fitbit / Google Health or WHOOP. Depending on your provider, these include steps, distance, calories, workouts, strain, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep sessions and stages, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, temperature and fitness estimates. These are provider readings and summaries, not raw physical sensor signals. Sentra derives daily features, personal baselines, provisional indicators and group statistics. Missing readings remain missing.

For sign-in and account linking, Sentra checks the provider's stable identifier and retains a provider-specific keyed hash. Names and email addresses returned by WHOOP for this check are discarded. Passwords remain with the provider. Authorization credentials are held separately in the protected collector store.

Location, GPS routes, contacts, messages, names, email addresses and provider identifiers are excluded from wellness records, organization views and exports. Original provider responses remain private for audit and can contain identifying fields; only authorized Sentra operators can open them.

Who can see what

  • Group-only sharing is the default. Organization users see nothing individual about you. Group statistics appear only when a statistic has at least five eligible contributors.
  • Individual sharing is optional. Authorized officers and reviewers in your named organization may see your pseudonymous profile, trends, indicators and permitted follow-up notes, as well as group results.
  • Sentra operators access information needed to operate, audit and recover the system. Access is limited to the authorized project team.
  • Demonstration use needs a separate choice. Pseudonymous real data appears in internal demonstrations only with permission. Real-data screenshots are not included in public project material. A new recipient organization requires fresh consent.

You may request your own data. Follow-up notes can contain free text about individuals or groups and are restricted by organization, role and current sharing permission.

Sharing changes and withdrawal

Sharing changes apply on the next page load. On withdrawal, collection for you stops, your information disappears from organization views and new reports, and you stop contributing to new group statistics. Provider authorization is revoked or left to expire. Previously delivered reports and downloads cannot be recalled.

Under the agreed interim policy, within 30 days of withdrawal an administrator reviews the affected records and deletes your detailed series, original responses and individual follow-ups, and removes your daily features from published outputs. Consent and withdrawal records are retained. Group follow-ups remain; group statistics already published from at least five people are not recomputed. Your information leaves backups as they expire, within at most 90 days.

Retention

  • Detailed readings have a 30-day readily available window; compressed archives preserve detail for evaluation.
  • Daily history and sparse daily/session data are kept through the final-year project evaluation, planned for May 2027. Original responses and follow-up history are retained for the project, subject to the withdrawal rules above.
  • The latest three verified encrypted backups are retained, each for at most 90 days. Backup keys are kept separately.
  • Recipients delete authorized exports within 30 days.
  • Consent history is retained for at least three years after the project ends. Final project-close handling of other data and long-term custody are recorded separately.

Deletion is a deliberate, reviewed administrative step. Publishing this notice does not itself change collection or delete records.

Contact

For data access, sharing, withdrawal or privacy questions, contact sentra.teamhub@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or detailed health records by email.
