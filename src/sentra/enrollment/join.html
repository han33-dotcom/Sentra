Share sleep, activity, heart-rate and recovery information from your wearable with your organization. Detailed readings are kept privately by the Sentra team.

What information is included?

Depending on your device: steps, activity and workouts, sleep and stages, heart rate (including detailed API readings), resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen, temperature and provider recovery scores. Sentra calculates personal comparisons and provisional wellness indicators.

Sentra does not request your location, GPS routes, contacts or messages. Your provider account identifier is checked privately to recognize your connection; names and email addresses returned by that check are discarded. Your provider password stays with the provider.
